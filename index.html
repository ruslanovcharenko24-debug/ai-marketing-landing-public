The Process

How it works

A simple four-step process from submission to structured diagnosis.

01

Complete the diagnostic form

The business owner fills in a professional intake form covering business context, marketing challenges, digital presence, competitors, and goals. You share only what you are comfortable sharing.

02

The system analyses the information

AI-assisted analysis and human strategic review are applied to the submitted information. The quality of the analysis depends on the richness of the context provided.

03

You receive a structured marketing diagnosis

A structured, first-level diagnosis is prepared covering the most significant weak points, opportunities, and practical first recommendations.

04

Further strategy or automation if needed

If the diagnosis reveals significant opportunity, a full AI Growth Audit or deeper consulting engagement may be offered as a next step.
